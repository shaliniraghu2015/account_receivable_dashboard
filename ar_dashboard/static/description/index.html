Account Receivable Dashboard

Monitor customer outstanding payments, overdue invoices, payment trends, and receivable performance from a single dashboard. This module provides real-time insights into your account receivables, helping your finance team improve cash flow, reduce overdue payments, and make informed financial decisions.

Overview

The Accounts Receivable Dashboard is a powerful Odoo module that provides a centralized view of your customer receivables. It helps accountants and finance teams monitor outstanding invoices, payment status, overdue amounts, and receivable trends without navigating through multiple reports.

The dashboard presents key financial metrics using KPI cards, interactive charts, and graphical reports, allowing users to quickly identify pending collections and make informed business decisions.
